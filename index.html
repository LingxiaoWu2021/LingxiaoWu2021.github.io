Welcome! I am Lingxiao Wu (Leo), an Assistant Professor in the Department of Aeronautical and Aviation Engineering at The Hong Kong Polytechnic University (PolyU), where I also lead the MSc programme in Aviation Engineering and Operations Management. My research combines operations research and data analytics to solve planning and operations problems in aviation, low-altitude and maritime transportation. My work has appeared in journals including Operations Research, Transportation Science, INFORMS Journal on Computing and Transportation Research Part B; the full list is on the publications page.

Before joining PolyU in 2022, I was a postdoctoral research fellow at HEC Montréal, Canada, working with Professors Yossiri Adulyasak and Jean-François Cordeau as a GERAD postdoctoral fellow. I received my Ph.D. from PolyU in 2020 as a Hong Kong PhD Fellowship awardee, my M.S. from Tongji University and my B.S. from Dalian Maritime University.

Prospective Ph.D. students

I recruit Ph.D. students all year round. Self-motivated students with strong academic backgrounds in engineering, science or business who would like to pursue a Ph.D. in aviation, transportation or operations research are warmly encouraged to contact me at lingxiao-leo.wu [at] polyu.edu.hk.

Students in the group are supported by PolyU Ph.D. scholarships, publish in journals such as Transportation Science and Transportation Research Part B, and go on exchange study at institutions including HEC Montréal, the University of Toronto, the University of British Columbia, Nanyang Technological University and Loyola University Chicago. Meet the research team.

Research interests

My research focuses on, but is not limited to:

  • Aviation operations, e.g. airline fleet management, air cargo network design and fleet scheduling, aircraft maintenance planning, aircraft landing scheduling and air traffic management
  • Low-altitude economy and UAV service network design, e.g. drone and truck-and-drone delivery, urban air mobility, vertiport location, and drones for emergency response
  • Shipping and port operations, e.g. berth allocation, vessel service planning, pilotage and towage, liner and bulk shipping, and port electrification
  • Data-driven optimization under uncertainty, e.g. stochastic programming, robust and distributionally robust optimization, and predict-then-optimize
  • Large-scale exact algorithms, e.g. column generation, branch-and-price and branch-and-cut
